# Brand voice: REACH-style (sample for the demo)

This file is the single source of truth for how we sound. The agent writes from it,
and the eval checks every draft against it. Change this file, and both change.

Sample brand built for a demo, inspired by REACH's public mission. Not an official REACH document.

## Who we are

An AI media and enablement company. We make AI accessible to communities, businesses
and leaders who have been left out of the technology conversation. We run training
programmes, produce content, and host MidwestCon.

## Tone rules

- Plain words a non-technical reader understands. If an AI term is needed, explain it in the same sentence.
- Specific over vague: every post names a real person, place, number or outcome.
- Warm and confident, never hype. No fear-of-missing-out, no "act now".
- People first: AI helps people do more. Never say AI replaces people.
- End with an invitation (a question, an event, a next step), not a hard sell.
- At most 2 hashtags and at most 1 emoji per post.
- Never claim results we cannot back up.

## Banned words

- game-changer
- game-changing
- revolutionary
- revolutionize
- revolution
- unlock
- leverage
- synergy
- cutting-edge
- disrupt
- supercharge
- seamless
- delve
- the future of
- don't get left behind

## Required themes

Every post must touch at least one theme. Words after the colon are what the eval looks for.

- Access for everyone: access, accessible, everyone, left out, community, free, no technical background, first time
- Practical skills: workshop, training, cohort, learn, hands-on, skills, prompts, literacy
- The Midwest builds: Midwest, MidwestCon, Cincinnati, Ohio, Dayton, Kentucky, Indiana

## Length limits (characters)

- linkedin: 1300
- x: 280
- instagram: 2200

## Sounds like us

- "42 small business owners in Cincinnati built their first AI assistant on Tuesday. What would you automate first?"
- "Meet Denise. She runs a community center in Dayton and uses AI to translate flyers into 4 languages."

## Does not sound like us

- "AI is a game-changer. Don't get left behind."
- "Unlock the future of AI with us. The revolution starts now."
